august-26-2026 - why i am learning to program again

At a very young age I had been exposed to programming by my father who is a software engineer. During this time I viewed it as something that I had to do as a part of my future career and to make my parents happy. As you may expect, this didn't exactly make my younger self view coding as something fun, or even something that I wanted to do as a career. As the years passed I always tried to get back into coding again but nothing ever worked. My younger frame of mind of coding was still stuck in my brain: "This is boring, lets do something fun!" fast forward to a few months ago I tried for the millioneth time to try and learn to code again. during this time I had just taken ap computer science principles and enjoyed the class for the most part so maybe that was part of it. This time, instead of watching tutorials and doing mundane, boring, coding activies, I instead tried to make something about something that I cared about. for me that was high jump. So I created a high high tracker and kept implementing new concepts and ideas that I learned about. This kept me programming and doing something that I cared about. In my opinion this is the key to learning how to program or to make yourself want to do anything in life. Make it enjoyable. When you make it enjoyable you will end up looking forward to it which will speed up your progress exponentioally vs if you tried to force yourself like I did. Programming is such a valuable skill and even if you career has nothing to do with programming, the skills that you will learn if you dedicate even just a little bit of your time to coding will benefit your life so much.
